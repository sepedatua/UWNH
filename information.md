## 3. Recruitment Mechanics & Tips

1. **Pub / Tavern Interaction**:
   - Walk into the port's **Pub** (Building #4).
   - If an unemployed mate is present in town, a **"Recruit Mate"** button will appear in the facility dialog.
   - Clicking it opens their authentic 16-bit appraisal modal showing age, stats, level, skill badges, and signing bonus.
2. **Treating to Drinks (Morale Discount)**:
   - Buying a round of wine or ale for the tavern patrons lowers the mate's required Fame threshold by **25%**, making proud or high-tier officers significantly easier to hire early in the game.
3. **Waitress Gossip & Rumors**:
   - Speak with the tavern **Waitress** and choose **"Gossip & Rumors"**.
   - If no hireable officer is in the current tavern, the waitress will give you intelligence on the exact port where the nearest unhired mate was last sighted.
4. **Wages & Loyalty**:
   - Upon recruitment, the signing bonus is deducted from your gold.
   - Monthly wages are paid out periodically from your ship's treasury; keep your gold reserves healthy to preserve high fleet morale.

## 4. Bridge Officer Posts & Duty Assignment

Once hired, open the **Fleet / Mates** menu to assign officers to critical shipboard roles:

* **Consort Ship Captains (Ships #2–#10)**:
  - Appoint a mate to command additional vessels in your squadron.
  - Improves ship maneuverability, combat response, and independence during naval engagements.
* **First Mate**:
  - Second-in-command of the flagship.
  - Advises the Commodore on harbor readiness, provisions, and danger warnings.
* **Chief Navigator**:
  - Oversees celestial observations and latitude/longitude bearings.
  - Displays the navigator's name and coordinates on the bottom-left sextant of the Sailing HUD.
* **Bookkeeper**:
  - Financial officer.
  - Lowers transaction tariffs and monitors commodity prices in markets.
* **Chief Lookout**:
  - Stations at the crow's nest.
  - Expands the visual Fog of War line-of-sight horizon for spotting uncharted villages, supply havens, shoals, and enemy sails.
* **Reserve Navigator**:
  - Off-duty companion awaiting fleet reassignment or future consort command.

# PART II: SHIPS, SHIPYARDS & INVESTMENT GUIDE

## 1. How Shipyard Investment Works

In *Uncharted Waters: New Horizons*, the types and quantity of ships available in a port's shipyard depend directly on the port's **Industry Value** (ranging from **100 to 1,000 points**).

### Investment Rules & Mechanics
* **Where to Invest**: Enter the local **Shipyard** (Building #3 in town) and select the **"Invest"** option.
* **Investment Increments**: You can invest **500 G**, **1,000 G**, **2,500 G**, or **5,000 G** in a single transaction (repeatable as long as you have funds).
* **Conversion Rate**: **100 Gold = +1 Industry Point** (`+10 Industry per 1,000 Gold`).
* **Investment Turnaround / Duration**: **Instantaneous**.
  - Unlike the 1993 floppy disk editions where investments took 1 in-game month to mature, in this remake the local industry points update **immediately upon transaction**.
  - The shipyard inventory cache resets on the spot, so newly unlocked high-tier ship hulls become available to buy right away during the same port visit!
* **Inventory Stock Scale**:
  - **Industry < 400**: 2 ships in stock.
  - **Industry 400–600**: 2–4 ships in stock.
  - **Industry 600–800**: 2–6 ships in stock.
  - **Industry > 800**: 2–8 ships in stock.
* **Multiplayer Bonus**: In Multiplayer Mode (`mp_endless`), shipyard investment raises your nation's port industrial hegemony, contributing to national sovereignty and investment dividends.

## 3. Ships Available Without Investment vs. With Investment

### A. Available Immediately WITHOUT Investment (Early to Mid Game)

Major capitals start with baseline industry ratings between **600 and 780**. Without spending a single coin on investment, you can immediately find and buy:

1. **Lightweight & Starter Ships (Industry 100–300)**:
   * **Balsa, Hansa Cog, Light Galley, Tallette, Caravela Latina, Caravela Redonda, Dhow, Kansen**.
2. **Intermediate Explorers & Merchants (Industry 400–500)**:
   * **Brigantine** (Barcelona / Lisbon / Seville).
   * **Flemish Galleon** (Lisbon / Seville / Genoa / Marseille).
   * **Nao** (Available in almost all European capitals: Lisbon, Seville, London, Amsterdam, Genoa, Venice).
   * **Xebec** (Istanbul, Alexandria, Algiers).
   * **Atakabune & Junk** (Japan and China baseline ports).
3. **Heavy Mid-Game Transports (Industry 500–700)**:
   * **Venetian Galeass** (Industry 500): Available immediately in **Genoa** (750) and **Venice** (720).
   * **Carrack** (Industry 600): Available immediately in **Lisbon** (770), **Seville** (750), **Genoa** (750), **Amsterdam** (720), **London** (650), and **Istanbul** (680).
   * **Buss** (Industry 700): Available immediately in **Genoa** (750) and **Venice** (720).

### B. High-Tier Ships Requiring Industrial Investment

To unlock the most powerful warships and colossal trade flagships, you must invest gold at the local shipyard:

1. **Galleon (Industry Req: 800 — Cost: 60,000 G)**:
   * **Where to Invest**: Lisbon, Seville, London, Amsterdam, Antwerp, or New World hubs.
   * **Investment Needed**:
     * In **Lisbon** (Base 770): Needs only **+30 Industry** (Cost: **3,000 G**).
     * In **Seville** (Base 750): Needs only **+50 Industry** (Cost: **5,000 G**).
     * In **London** (Base 650): Needs **+150 Industry** (Cost: **15,000 G**).
2. **Sloop (Industry Req: 850 — Cost: 16,000 G)**:
   * **Where to Invest**: **London** or **Bristol** (Shipyard Archetype #2).
   * **Investment Needed**: Requires bringing English shipyards from 650 to 850 (Cost: **20,000 G**).
3. **Frigate (Industry Req: 1,000 — Cost: 224,000 G)**:
   * **Where to Invest**: **London**, **Bristol**, **Amsterdam**, or **Antwerp**.
   * **Investment Needed**: Max out industry to 1,000 (Cost: **28,000 G to 35,000 G** in capital investment).
4. **Barge (Industry Req: 1,000 — Cost: 300,000 G)**:
   * **Where to Invest**: **London** or **Bristol** (Shipyard Archetype #2).
   * **Stats**: 1,000 capacity, 120 cannons, 450 crew. Requires 1,000 Industry in English ports.
5. **Full-rigged Ship (Industry Req: 1,000 — Cost: 320,000 G)**:
   * **Where to Invest**: **Amsterdam** or **Antwerp** (Shipyard Archetype #3).
   * **Stats**: The largest Western vessel in the game. 1,200 capacity, 150 cannons, 500 crew. Requires 1,000 Industry in Dutch / Hanseatic ports.
6. **Tekkousen (Industry Req: 1,000 — Cost: 140,000 G)**:
   * **Where to Invest**: **Nagasaki** or **Sakai** (Shipyard Archetype #10, Japan).
   * **Stats**: Legendary iron-armored floating fortress. 1,100 capacity, 100 cannons, 90 durability. Immune to cannon fire combustion. Requires investing Japanese ports from 350 to 1,000 (Cost: **65,000 G**).

## 5. Ship Purchase & Remodeling Requirements

1. **Available Officer (Captaincy)**:
   - When buying a consort ship (Ship #2 through #10), you must have at least one unassigned navigator in your mates roster to assume captaincy of the vessel.
2. **Fleet Capacity**:
   - A fleet can comprise up to **10 ships maximum**. If your fleet already contains 10 vessels, you must sell or scuttle an existing hull before buying another.
3. **Remodeling (Refitting Hulls & Rechristening)**:
   - In any Shipyard, select **"Remodel"** for a nominal **200 Gold** shipyard fee.
   - **Ship Name (Rechristen)**: You can change/rename any ship in your fleet (up to 24 characters) during remodel.
   - You can redistribute the vessel's total capacity between:
     - **Crew Quarters**: Must stay between `minimumCrew` and `maximumCrew`.
     - **Gun Batteries**: Up to `maximumGuns`.
     - **Cargo Hold**: The remaining capacity becomes trade goods and provisions space (`Total Capacity - Crew - Guns`).
